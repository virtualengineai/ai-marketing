Here's a framework I use to audit any business's revenue operations in under 30 minutes.

I call it the FLOW Check. It reveals exactly where leads are falling through the cracks.

F — First Response Time
How long does it take to contact a new lead? If it's over 5 minutes, you're losing money. Full stop.

L — Lead Source Tracking
Do you know which channels are actually generating revenue — not just traffic? If not, you're flying blind.

O — Offer Clarity
When a lead lands on your site or talks to your team, is it crystal clear what you do, who you help, and what they should do next? Confusion kills conversions.

W — Workflow Automation
What happens after a lead opts in? Is there a structured sequence, or does it depend on someone remembering to follow up?

Run your business through these four checkpoints.

Most of the time, one of these is the primary bottleneck — and fixing it alone can move revenue meaningfully.
