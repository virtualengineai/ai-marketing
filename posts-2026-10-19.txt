CRM adoption is one of the biggest challenges I see in small and mid-sized businesses.

You buy the tool. You set it up. Six months later, nobody's actually using it — and leads are still getting tracked in a spreadsheet or someone's memory.

Here's why CRM adoption fails and how to fix it:

**Why it fails:**
• Too many manual steps required to update records
• No clear process for how leads should move through the pipeline
• The tool isn't connected to where leads actually come in
• No accountability for keeping the pipeline clean

**How to fix it:**
• Automate every data entry that can be automated (form captures, call logging, email tracking)
• Build a simple, visual pipeline that reflects your actual sales process
• Integrate your CRM with your website, ads, and communication tools
• Set a weekly "pipeline review" that takes 20 minutes to run

The CRM only works if it's being used. And people only use it if it makes their life easier, not harder.
