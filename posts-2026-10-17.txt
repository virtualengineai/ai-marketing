Most businesses measure "leads generated." Very few measure "leads converted."

That second number is where the money is.

Here's a quick framework I use to diagnose conversion leaks in any sales pipeline:

**1. Response speed** — How long does it take to contact a new lead? Anything over 5 minutes sees a dramatic drop in conversion probability.

**2. Contact attempts** — Are you reaching out once or following up across multiple touchpoints? Data shows 80% of sales require 5+ contacts.

**3. Lead tracking** — Can you see exactly where every lead is in your pipeline at this moment? If not, deals are falling through the cracks.

**4. Nurture sequences** — What happens to leads who aren't ready to buy today? A structured sequence keeps you top of mind without manual effort.

If you score poorly on any of these, you don't have a marketing problem. You have a systems problem.

The fix is simpler than most people think — and the ROI is immediate.
